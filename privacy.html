LifeShelf for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

This policy explains how LifeShelf handles information when you use the app. LifeShelf is a personal archive for files, notes, identity documents, payment cards and contacts.

Information you add

Files, photos, notes, contact details, card details and identity-document details that you enter or import are stored in LifeShelf's private app storage on your device. Private-space content is encrypted on the device. LifeShelf does not operate an account system or a server that receives your archive.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive and start a backup, LifeShelf requests the drive.appdata permission. It creates a complete archive, encrypts it on your device with AES-GCM before upload, and stores that encrypted file in LifeShelf's hidden Google Drive app-data folder. The archive may contain the items and attachments you chose to keep in LifeShelf, including private-space items when the private space is unlocked.

You can also opt into daily, weekly or monthly automatic backups. LifeShelf checks whether a backup is due when you open the app and uploads it if Google Drive access is already authorized. If you have a private space, LifeShelf waits until you unlock it before including its contents. Automatic backups do not run while the app is closed or while the private space is locked.

  • Your backup recovery code is the key needed to decrypt a backup. LifeShelf does not upload or share that code with Google Drive.
  • Google processes the authorization and stores the encrypted backup under Google's privacy terms. The app-data folder is hidden from the regular Drive file list and is accessible to LifeShelf through the permission you grant.
  • Backups are optional. Manual backups upload when you request them; automatic backups upload only when enabled, due, and the app is open (with the private space unlocked when configured). You can revoke LifeShelf's Google access in your Google Account and remove the app's Drive data using Google's app-data controls.
  • If you lose the recovery code, LifeShelf cannot decrypt a backup for you. Keep a copy somewhere safe and separate from the device.

Google Drive API information: Application data folder.

Update checks

When you choose Check for updates, LifeShelf contacts the public LifeShelf Releases service on GitHub to check for release information. If you choose to download an update, the APK is downloaded from GitHub Releases. These requests do not include your LifeShelf archive or backup recovery code. GitHub may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Sharing and sale

LifeShelf does not sell your archive information. The app does not send archive contents to the developer. When you enable a feature that uses a third-party service, such as Google Drive backup or checking GitHub for updates, the information needed for that request is handled by that service as described above and under its own policies.

Retention and deletion

Local items remain in the app until you delete them or clear/uninstall the app. Drive backups remain in your Google account until you remove LifeShelf's app data through Google's controls. Removing a local item does not automatically remove previously created backups; create a new backup if you want the latest archive to reflect your changes.

Children

LifeShelf is a general personal archive and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if LifeShelf's data practices change. The effective date above shows when the current version took effect.
